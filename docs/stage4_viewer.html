Shelter evaluation

Chennai does not break evenly. When the network fractures, some areas hold. Within the flood-free zones, blocks were evaluated by population density, local accessibility (NAINr800) and urban-scale accessibility (NAINr3000). Two safe zones stand out.

Cluster 7 is small, only 90 blocks, but spatially tight and highly connected. A place where resources can be concentrated and people can actually reach them.

Cluster 9 is the opposite: 50,424 blocks spread across all five urban fragments, a distributed backbone that keeps resilience within reach for a wider population.

Together they are complementary. But the shelters do not follow this logic. 48.6% of Chennai's emergency shelters sit more than 400 metres from any accessible safe zone, beyond reach when it matters most.
