Floodplain & density strategy

Using a DEM (Copernicus GLO-30) and waterway data (IWRS), the city's lowest-lying areas were cross-referenced with high flood-risk zones. Large open spaces within this overlap are proposed for floodplain excavation (blue), restoring natural drainage capacity, excluding streets with high local integration (NAINr800) to preserve the existing pedestrian network. Areas where residential buildings (Google Open Buildings) coincide with high flood-risk are designated for density reduction (orange), limiting future exposure through adaptive urban planning.
